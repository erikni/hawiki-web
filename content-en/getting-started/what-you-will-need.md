## Basic shopping list

1. A device on which to run Home Assistant.
2. Your home router and a spare wired network connection for the chosen controller.
3. One supported smart device, such as a smart plug for a lamp.
4. A phone or computer for the initial setup.

## The simplest route: Home Assistant Green

[Home Assistant Green](/buying-guide/home-assistant-green/) comes with Home Assistant already installed. Its manufacturer recommends it as an easy way to get started. Connect it to your router with a network cable, then connect the power supply.

To connect Zigbee devices, you will also need a compatible adapter such as [Home Assistant Connect ZBT-2](/buying-guide/home-assistant-connect-zbt-2/). Do not buy a sensor simply because its packaging calls it smart.

## What do you already have?

Before buying anything, note the brand and exact model number of the devices you already own. Check the [list of supported integrations](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/) to see what can be connected.

## Planning your budget

Allow for the controller, your first device and any required adapter. Check current prices, delivery charges and warranty terms with retailers in your country. This guide does not reproduce unverified offers or prices.
