## Purpose first, then product
Write down exactly what you want to solve. You need a different device to measure temperature than you do to control heating.

## Check these five things
1. **Specific model:** Is support for Home Assistant listed in the documentation?
2. **Connection:** Does the device require another hub or adapter?
3. **Internet:** Does it function locally, or is it dependent on the manufacturer's service?
4. **Power:** Does it use batteries, a mains socket, or does it require hardwiring?
5. **Total cost:** How much does the device, accessories, and any required subscription cost?

## What does Zigbee mean?
It is a method by which some sensors, buttons, and lights communicate wirelessly. You connect it to Home Assistant via a compatible adapter. The Zigbee label itself does not guarantee support for all functions of a specific model.

## What does integration mean?
A connection that allows Home Assistant to understand a device or service. Before purchasing, read the requirements and limitations of the relevant integration.

## Recommendation for a first purchase
Start with a single device that does not require electrical wiring modifications. Choose a specific product according to current documentation and availability in CZ/SK. Leave hardwiring to an electrician.
