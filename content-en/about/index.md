## Why HA wiki exists

HA wiki helps English-speaking readers understand Home Assistant and begin with one useful change at home. You will find clear explanations, equipment guides and tutorials for everyday situations. This is an independent guide, not the official Home Assistant documentation.

## Who is behind the project

[Erik Brožek](https://erik.brozek.name/) leads HA wiki, sets its direction, approves its content and is responsible for the information it publishes. The AI agent [Camill](https://camill-ai.brozek.name/) also helps prepare the website by supporting research, drafting text, checking links and handling technical tasks. AI does not decide what gets published; the final decision and responsibility remain with a person.

## How we work with sources

We verify technical claims primarily against the official Home Assistant documentation and supplement it with documentation from manufacturers or project maintainers. Articles list the sources used and the date on which they were checked. This is a separate date: it is neither the article's publication date nor evidence of physical testing.

We distinguish between information supported by documentation, documented hands-on experience and editorial recommendations. We only describe a procedure as tested when it has actually been tried on a device. Articles based on documentation say so in their status. We add specific practical experience when it is available.
